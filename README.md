# 🇸🇱 Cybersecurity and Crime Act 2021 — A Child-Friendly Guide

## Making Sierra Leone's Cyber Law Easier to Understand

As part of **Cybersecurity Awareness Month**, the Cybersecurity Unit of the **ICT Department, Parliament of Sierra Leone** has developed this educational resource to help children and young people better understand Sierra Leone's **Cybersecurity and Crime Act, 2021**.

The goal is to make important concepts contained in the Cybersecurity and Crime Act easier to understand by presenting them in simple, accessible and age-appropriate language.

---

## 🎯 Purpose

Cybersecurity is not only about technology. It is also about understanding our rights and responsibilities when using digital technologies.

This resource aims to:

- 📖 Introduce young people to Sierra Leone's cybercrime legislation
- 🔐 Promote safer and more responsible use of technology
- ⚖️ Help children understand that online activities can have legal consequences
- 🌐 Promote responsible digital citizenship
- 🧑‍🏫 Support teachers, parents and schools in cybersecurity education
- 🇸🇱 Contribute to national cybersecurity awareness and digital resilience

---

## 👦👧 Who Is This Resource For?

This resource is primarily designed for:

- Children and young people
- Students
- Teachers and educators
- Parents and guardians
- Youth organisations
- Schools and educational institutions
- Community awareness programmes
- Cybersecurity awareness practitioners

It can also be used by anyone who wants a simpler introduction to key concepts relating to cybercrime and cyber law in Sierra Leone.

---

## 📚 What You Will Learn

The guide introduces young readers to concepts such as:

- What cybercrime means
- Responsible use of computers and digital devices
- Online safety
- Unlawful activities in cyberspace
- Protecting personal information
- Respecting other people's privacy
- Responsible use of social media
- Consequences of certain cyber-related activities
- The importance of reporting cyber incidents
- Responsible digital citizenship

---

## ⚖️ About the Cybersecurity and Crime Act, 2021

The **Cybersecurity and Crime Act, 2021** provides Sierra Leone's legal framework for addressing various forms of cybercrime and cybersecurity-related matters.

This project does **not replace the official Act**.

Instead, it is an educational initiative intended to make selected concepts easier for young people and members of the public to understand.

For legal interpretation, enforcement, or official reference, readers should consult the **official Cybersecurity and Crime Act, 2021** and seek appropriate professional or institutional guidance where necessary.

## ⚠️ Educational Disclaimer

This repository is provided **for educational and public-awareness purposes only**.

The child-friendly guide is a simplified educational interpretation of selected concepts relating to the **Cybersecurity and Crime Act, 2021**. It is not intended to replace the official legislation, provide legal advice, or serve as an authoritative legal interpretation.

In the event of any difference between this educational material and the official legislation, the **official Cybersecurity and Crime Act, 2021** takes precedence.

Readers should consult the official legislation and qualified legal professionals or appropriate authorities where legal advice or interpretation is required.
